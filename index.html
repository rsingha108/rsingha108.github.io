About

I am a fifth-year PhD student in Computer Science at UCLA, co-advised by Todd Millstein and George Varghese. I work on making network software and configurations correct: fast what-if analysis of configuration changes and failures (incremental control-plane simulation in Batfish), testing protocol implementations with generative AI and symbolic execution, and synthesizing verifiably correct, robust implementations using modularity. Along the way I build agentic, multi-agent LLM pipelines that do this work automatically.

My tools have found dozens of previously unknown bugs in widely used DNS, BGP, and other protocol implementations. I have interned at Microsoft Research (Redmond, twice, and Bangalore) and Intel.

  • Network verification
  • Protocol testing
  • Control-plane simulation
  • Symbolic execution
  • LLM agents for systems

I am on the job market. I graduate in Spring 2027 and am looking for full-time research scientist roles. Feel free to reach me at {{ site.email }}.
